Start here

Before you start

Rhythm Plus is firmware for the Novation Circuit Rhythm. It starts from the official firmware 2.0.1 and keeps how the unit works; the new features sit on top. This manual shows only what Rhythm Plus adds. For everything else, read Novation's Circuit Rhythm User Guide.

1. Back up the SD card

Open the Rhythm Plus app (rhythm_plus_app.html) in Chrome or Edge. Its Backup page copies the packs and the RP_AUTO and RP_INTERNAL folders to the computer and checks the copy. Open the card's top folder, so that the app sees all three. Restore puts a backup back on the card.

2. Install or update

  1. In the app, open the Firmware page and choose a build: Stable is tested on the unit, In development is the newest.
  2. Download Circuit Rhythm 2.0.1 (5706) from Novation and drop it on the page. The page adds the chosen build to it.
  3. Connect the unit over USB and turn it on. Close Novation Components. Press Connect.
  4. Press Install. When the unit asks, press Play on it. Do not disconnect it until the end.

To go back to the official firmware, drop Novation's own .syx file in the box "Your .syx file" and install it, or use Novation Components.

Tutorial 1

Your first mutate

About 2 minutes. You need a project with a few patterns on its tracks.

  1. Load the project. Press Patterns, then Play.
  2. Hold Shift and tap Duplicate. Each track gets a new play list of its own patterns. Each list starts at the end of the track's pattern.
  3. Listen. Tap Duplicate again, with Shift still held, for new lists.
  1. Hold Shift and hold Duplicate for a moment: the Mutate settings open. Tap a pad in the Length column for a longer or shorter list. Tap Duplicate to close the settings. The next mutate uses them.
  2. To go back to your chains, hold Clear and tap Duplicate.
Tip: do you like the lists? Hold Shift and tap a scene pad: the scene keeps each track's mutate (chapter 5).

Tutorial 2

Your first Granular sound

About 3 minutes. A long sample works best: a pad, a voice or a loop.

  1. Select track 1 and choose a long sample in Sample View.
  2. Tap track button 1 again: the Track Parameter Editor opens. Tap pad 27: the Granular page.
  3. Hold pad 27 for a moment: its Settings open. Tap pad 17: it turns green, Granular is on. Tap pad 27 to close the Settings.
  1. Play notes, or press Play if the track has steps. Turn K2 (Size) and K3 (Density): you hear the grains change. Turn K1 (Position) to move through the sample.
  2. Turn K5 (Position scatter) and K6 (Pitch scatter) for a cloud. For one held moment, open the Settings and tap pad 23: Freeze.
Tip: the knobs work when Granular is off too. Their values stay for when you turn it on.

Tutorial 3

Fit a loop to the tempo

About 2 minutes. You need a drum loop or a break as a sample.

  1. Select the track and choose the loop in Sample View.
  2. Press Sample two times: Sample Mode opens.
  3. Tap the length of the loop: pad 13 for 1 bar, pad 14 for 2 bars. Pad 9 turns green: stretch is on.
  1. Press Play. Change the tempo: the loop stays in time, and its pitch does not change.
  2. Tap pad 9 to hear the loop at its own speed again: pad 9 turns red. Tap it again and the length comes back.
Tip: a dim red length pad is a length the sample cannot reach: it would play more than 4 times slower or faster.

To play only a part of the loop, tap pad 10 (amber): K2 and K3 then choose the start and the end that fill the length (chapter 16).

Tutorial 4

A keyboard and a second unit

Play a synth or a second unit from a keyboard through the Rhythm, in time with the Rhythm's clock. You need a MIDI keyboard, a second MIDI device and two MIDI cables.

  1. Connect the cables as in the drawing.
  2. Hold Shift while you turn the Rhythm on: Advanced Setup opens.
  3. Press Duplicate until it is yellow: Merge. Press Play to leave Advanced Setup.
  1. Hold Shift and press Save: Setup opens. Pad 32, Clock Tx, must be bright: on.
  2. Set the keyboard to a channel that no Rhythm track uses, for example 9. Or tap pad 25 in Setup: Note Rx off. Then the Rhythm's tracks do not play the keyboard. Press Play to leave Setup.
  3. On the second unit, turn on its clock sync and set its sound to the keyboard's channel.
  4. Press Play on the Rhythm. The second unit follows its tempo, and the keyboard plays it.

Tutorial 5

Record a Granular knob on a lane

About 3 minutes. A Granular knob records its moves on one of the track's 8 automation lanes. Here, Size (K2) gets lane K6. Do Tutorial 2 first, so that Granular is on.

  1. Open the Track Parameter Editor and tap pad 27: the Granular page.
  2. Hold pad 27 for a moment: Settings open, and pad 27 pulses.
  3. Press Down (lit white): Assignments open.
  4. Turn K2 a little. Its light goes white: Size is the picked control. Its value does not change.
  5. Tap pad 22: lane K6. It turns bright lime. Size now records on lane K6.
  1. Press Up to go back to Settings, then tap pad 27 to close them.
  2. Press Record (●), then Play. Turn K2 while the pattern plays: the bar is red, and the moves go into the steps.
  3. Press Record again to stop recording. The pattern now plays your Size moves.
Tip: choose a lane that has no Stock automation you want. Lane K6 is the Stock HP Filter's lane. That knob still works by hand, but it does not record while Size uses the lane.

To delete the moves, hold Clear and turn K2 left until it stops. To free the lane, open Assignments, pick K2 and tap pad 22 again.

Chapter 1

Pattern Mutate: overview and quick start

A mutate gives each track a new play list: a short run of that track's own patterns, in a random order. The track plays the list in a loop in place of its chain. Your patterns do not change. You can go back to the chains at any time.

Use it to find new variations of a groove: try a mutate, keep it if you like it, try another if you do not.

Quick start

  1. Press Patterns to show the Patterns View. Press Play.
  2. Hold Shift and tap Duplicate. Each track gets a new list. The lists start at each track's next pattern end.
  3. Tap again for another mutate. Each tap makes new lists.
  4. To go back to the chains: hold Clear and tap Duplicate.

The Duplicate button

On the Patterns View, Duplicate shows the state of the mutate:

DuplicateMeaning
Its normal lightNo button held: Duplicate copies patterns, as normal.
Dim greenShift is held: a tap mutates.
Full greenShift + Duplicate is down.
Green, pulsingThe Mutate settings page is open.
RedClear is held and a track has a mutate: a tap ends every mutate.

The settings

Hold Shift and hold Duplicate for a moment to open the Mutate settings page. Tap Duplicate to close it. Shift + tap Duplicate on the page makes a mutate and keeps the page open. The settings are for all tracks and are saved with the project. Chapters 2 and 3 show each setting.

A track's mutate stays until you end it, pick a pattern for that track, or change the scene (see chapter 5).

Each slot of a list either keeps the chain's pattern at that place, or gets a random one. The table shows a chain of 4 patterns.

For other lengths the number is rounded, and at least one slot changes. Slots past the end of the chain are always random. With Keep slot 1 On, Amount counts only the slots after slot 1.

Live (column 1)

Live lets you hear the settings while you change them. With Live Off, a change is used at the next mutate (Shift + tap Duplicate). With Live On, when you change Length, Length type, Pool, Amount, Repeats or Keep slot 1, the list of each track that mutates changes at once, while it plays. Tracks that do not mutate keep their lists.

The change is not a new random list. Each mutate gives each track a seed: a start number for its random choices. Live makes the list again from the same seed with the new setting. So when you set a setting back, the list that you had comes back. With Live On, setting When to Every chain end also starts it at once, as a tap does.

Scenes and saving

Scenes

A scene keeps the mutate of each track: its seed, the settings that made its list and its chain. So a scene plays the exact lists that you heard when you saved it, and Live goes on from them.

On the Mixer ViewResult
Shift + a scene padSaves the scene with each track's mutate.
A scene padPlays the scene with its saved lists. Stopped: at once. Playing: at the end of track 1's pattern. The mutate plays until then.
Duplicate + a scene pad, then anotherCopies the scene, its mutate too.
Clear + a scene padClears the scene, its mutate too.

A scene saved without a mutate plays its chains: a scene change ends the mutate of the tracks with no list in that scene.

In a scene chain, the chain goes to the next scene when each mutated track has played the last slot of its list. Play starts on the chain's first scene, with its own lists.

With Pool With steps, a list uses only the patterns that have notes. If you add notes to an empty pattern, or clear a pattern, a saved list can change.

Saving

The project keeps the Mutate settings, each track's mutate and the mutate of each scene. A project from an earlier firmware opens with the default settings and no mutate.

Chapter 7

Step Mutate: overview and the settings page

Use it to vary one track's pattern, for example new velocities, notes or gates for a hi-hat line.

Step Mutate changes the steps of the selected track's pattern: their notes, velocities, gates and more. It changes only the steps from the pattern's start step to its end step, so it works the same for 16 or 32 steps. Each track has its own settings.

Step Mutate changes the pattern itself. There is no undo. To keep the pattern as it is, duplicate it to an empty pattern on the Patterns View first, then mutate the copy.

Quick start

  1. Select a track: its Sample View or Note View shows.
  2. Hold Shift and tap Duplicate. The pattern's steps change places, as the standard Mutate does.
  3. Hold Shift and hold Duplicate for a moment. The settings page opens.
  4. Tap a target pad to turn it on or off. Hold it to see its settings on columns 6-8.
  5. Tap Duplicate to close the page. Hold Shift and tap Duplicate to mutate with the new settings.
PadsWhat they do
Pads 1-3, 9, 17, 18, 25-28The targets (chapter 8). Bright = on, dim = off. Tap to turn on or off. Hold to show the target's settings: they stay after you let go.
Columns 6-8The shown target's settings. A pad is one value. A meter fills from the bottom: more light, more change. A choice: the chosen value is full.
The other padsDark. They do nothing.

Stretch while playing, and Granular tracks

Stretch while the sample plays

You can turn stretch on and off while the sample plays. On: the sound goes on from where it is. Off: it goes on at the sample's own speed to the end of the note; the next note plays without stretch. Each change fades over about 5 ms, so it does not click.

Sample Mode on a Granular track

On a Granular track, pads 11-16 set Scan: the playhead moves through the part from K2 to K3 in that length, in time with the tempo. Tap the bright pad again and Scan goes back to its value from before. The sweeps stay in time, also when the tempo changes: each sweep ends on a whole number of its lengths, counted from bar 1. If you tap a pad part way through, the sweep ends at the next of these points, or at the one after it when less than half the length is left. When each note restarts the playhead (Triggered, or Retrigger on), each note plays one sweep. If you turn Scan, no length pad is chosen. The Scan length is kept apart from the stretch length: it stays when you turn Granular off and on again, and the project saves it. On a Granular track, pad 9 is red and pad 10 is dark. While Slice is on, pads 11-16 are dim red.

Pad 20 picks which sample sets the sweep's speed. White: the track's sample, the one chosen in Sample View. A flipped step's sample then moves at the same speed, so a shorter one plays through more than once in the length and a longer one is cut off at its end. Amber: each sample. Every note's own sample, flipped or not, moves through its part once in the length.

In One Shot mode, a Granular note plays until the playhead has moved through the part one time. If the sample at its own speed plays longer, the note lasts that long. Then the grains fade out, and no new grains start until the next note.

Scan starts in the middle, so the playhead stays still. Turn Scan back to the middle and the playhead goes back to the start of the part, unless Freeze is on. The middle is Scan 63 to 65, with any fine setting. The slower the Scan, the longer a One Shot note: near the middle a sweep through a short drum takes minutes.

Gates

A step's pads play for the step's gate. The next step does not cut them off: a long gate plays on under the next steps. If a step inside the gate (a step that the gate lights) has the same pad, the pad plays on; on the first step after the gate, it starts again.

Each effect type plays one effect at a time. When two steps use the same type, the newer step takes over with no restart (Beat Repeat goes from 1/16 to 1/4 and keeps playing). A step has one pad of each type: when you record or hold the step, a new pad replaces the pad of the same type.

Saving

The steps' effects are saved with the project, apart from the track's own data, so the track's samples and flips do not change. A project holds up to 47 different pad sets. A set is two or more pads on a step, or Random or Repeat; one pad on a step needs no set. When all 47 are in use, a step cannot get a new set and keeps what it had. A step that you delete in any view loses its effects. A pattern copy (Duplicate) copies the steps but not their Grid FX pads. Step Mutate moves the pads with their steps and automation. With sticky Shift on, Play continues as Shift + Play does. To leave, tap Shift alone (shorter than 0.3 s), or tap any other button except Record and Clear: it also does its job. Hold a button, or Shift + a button, to see its view; let go and the scrub comes back.

Sound and effects

With K5 green, each step that you turn to sounds while playback is stopped, until 120 ms after the last turn. While you scrub, the FX track plays no effects from its steps, and the Shift views do not open.

Use it to try changes on a copy and keep the original, without a computer.

Copy a project or a sample to another slot of the loaded pack. The internal memory is never changed. Stop playback first: while the unit plays, these pads do nothing.

Press Projects to see the projects. Duplicate is white on this page, and green while you hold it. Hold Duplicate for every step.

Chapter 22

MIDI Thru, Merge and USB merge

Use it to play other gear from one keyboard through the Rhythm, in time with its clock.

These are in Advanced Setup: hold Shift while you turn the unit on. The unit saves them when you change them. If it plays, it saves them when it stops.

The Thru jack: Duplicate

Press Duplicate to go to the next mode. Duplicate's light shows the mode.

ModeLightWhat goes out of the Thru jack
Thru (default)Dim redA copy of MIDI In. Nothing from the unit.
Out 2GreenThe unit's own MIDI, as from MIDI Out. Nothing from MIDI In.
MergeYellowMIDI In and the unit's own MIDI together.
FilterBlueMIDI In, but not the notes and controls on a channel that the unit listens on. Nothing from the unit.

USB merge: pad 23

Pad 23 green: MIDI In also goes to the computer over USB. Dim green: off.

Clock

With Clock Tx on, the clock, start, stop and continue that come in on MIDI In do not go out of the Thru jack in Merge, or over USB. The unit sends its own clock. With Clock Rx also on, the unit follows the incoming clock, so its clock has the same tempo. With Clock Tx off, the incoming clock goes out. In Filter the incoming clock always goes out. So only one clock goes out.

Example: a keyboard through the unit

Keyboard to MIDI In, the Thru jack to a second unit. Set the Thru jack to Merge. The second unit gets the keyboard's notes and this unit's clock. Tracks 1-8 use MIDI channels 1-8 at first, so a keyboard on channel 1 plays track 1 too. To keep the keyboard's notes off this unit's tracks, turn Note Rx off in Setup (Shift + Save, pad 25), or set the keyboard to a channel that no track uses. To send no notes from this unit, turn Note Tx off (pad 26). Clock Rx and Clock Tx are pads 31 and 32.

SysEx from MIDI In is not copied.

Tip: with Merge, set the keyboard to a channel that no track uses, for example 9-15. Then the tracks do not play it, and Note Rx can stay on.

Chapter 23

Quantize

Use it after you record live, to put the hits on the steps.

Press Pattern Settings on a track. Tap pad 25. The track's pattern is quantized at once, from its start step to its end step. It works while the pattern plays and while it is stopped.

A step hasAfter a quantize
One hit on microstep 1No change.
One hit on microstep 2 or 3The hit moves to microstep 1 of the same step.
One hit on microstep 4, 5 or 6The hit moves to microstep 1 of the next step. If that step is empty, the hit takes its note, velocity, gate, probability and sample flip with it. If that step has a note, it gets a hit on microstep 1.
Two or more hits (a roll)No change.

A late hit on the end step moves to the start step. Steps outside the start and end do not change. On the FX track, the Grid FX pads of a step move with its hit. Automation stays on its steps.

Pad 25Meaning
GreenReady.
Green, three flashesThe pattern is quantized.
Quantize changes the pattern itself. There is no undo. To keep the pattern as it is, duplicate it to an empty pattern on the Patterns View first.
