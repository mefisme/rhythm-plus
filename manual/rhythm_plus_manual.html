Chapter 1

Pattern Mutate: overview and quick start

A mutate gives each track a new play list: a short run of that track's own patterns, in a random order. The track plays the list in a loop in place of its chain. Your patterns do not change. You can go back to the chains at any time.

Use it to find new variations of a groove: try a mutate, keep it if you like it, try another if you do not.

Quick start

  1. Press Patterns to show the Patterns page. Press Play.
  2. Hold Shift and tap Duplicate. Each track gets a new list. The lists start at each track's next pattern end.
  3. Tap again for another mutate. Each tap makes new lists.
  4. To go back to the chains: hold Clear and Shift, and tap Duplicate.

The Duplicate button

On the Patterns page, Duplicate shows the state of the mutate:

DuplicateMeaning
Its normal lightNo button held: Duplicate copies patterns, as before.
Dim greenShift is held: a tap mutates.
Full greenShift + Duplicate is down.
Green, pulsingThe Mutate settings page is open.
RedClear and Shift are held: a tap ends every mutate.

The settings

Hold Shift and hold Duplicate for a moment to open the Mutate settings page. Tap Duplicate to close it. The settings are for all tracks and are saved with the project. Chapters 2 and 3 show each setting.

A track's mutate stays until you end it, pick a pattern for that track, or change the scene (see chapter 5).

Chapter 3

The Mutate settings, page 2

Press Down on the settings page. Column 3 goes on with Length 5-8. Columns 4 to 6 are three more settings. The greener colours tell you that you are on page 2.

ColumnSettingPads, top to bottomWhat it does
3Length5, 6, 7, 8The Length bar goes on from page 1.
4AmountAll (default) · Most · Some · A littleHow much of the chain a mutate changes. The meter fills from the bottom up: more light, more change.
5RepeatsOn (default) · OffOff: a list never plays the same pattern twice in a row (also not from its last slot back to slot 1).
6Keep slot 1Off (default) · OnOn: each list starts on the chain's first pattern, a home beat. Only the slots after it change.

Amount, step by step

Each slot of a list either keeps the chain's pattern at that place, or gets a random one. For a chain of 4 patterns:

AmountSlots that changeExample: chain 1 · 2 · 3 · 4
All4 of 47 · 6 · 2 · 5
Most3 of 45 · 2 · 8 · 6
Some2 of 41 · 6 · 3 · 2
A little1 of 41 · 2 · 7 · 4

For other lengths the number is rounded, and at least one slot changes. Slots past the end of the chain are always random. With Keep slot 1 On, Amount counts only the slots after slot 1.

With Repeats Off, a track that has only one pattern with notes still gets a list. It plays that pattern.

Chapter 4

Playing a mutate

On the Patterns page, each column is a track and each pad is a pattern. A mutated track shows its list:

You doResult
PlayEach mutated track starts on slot 1 of its list.
Shift + PlayPlay goes on from where you stopped. Then the list goes on from slot 1.
Mutate while stoppedThe mutated tracks show their whole new list. The pad of the old stop point shows again when you play.
Tap a pattern pad of a mutated trackThat track's mutate ends. The stock pattern pick plays, as before.
Clear + pad, Duplicate + padClear or copy a pattern, as before. The mutate stays.
Shift heldThe track buttons show their normal Shift lights. The pads keep showing the lists.

View Lock

Hold Shift and press Patterns to turn View Lock on or off. When you turn it on, the patterns that play at that moment turn white and stay white.

With View Lock onResult
Shift + a padThat track's white pad moves to this pattern. The mutate and the chain do not change.
A pad without ShiftThe track's mutate ends and the chain is picked, as before. The white pads stay.
Clear + a green track buttonThe track's mutate ends (chapter 5). The white pads stay.
View Lock off, then onThe white pads move to the patterns that play now.

Chapter 5

Ending a mutate, scenes and saving

See which tracks are mutated

Hold Clear on the Patterns page. The track buttons of the mutated tracks are green; the other track buttons are dark.

You doResult
Clear + a green track buttonThat track's mutate ends. Its button flashes red, then goes dark. The track goes back to its chain.
Clear + a dark track buttonNothing happens. The track is not selected.
Clear + Shift + DuplicateEvery mutate ends. Duplicate is red while Clear and Shift are held. The mutated tracks flash red.
Clear held on the settings pageThe pads show the Patterns page, so you can see each mutate. Duplicate keeps pulsing green, so you know that you are still on the settings page. The pads do nothing. Clear + a track button ends that track's mutate.

On a track page, Clear + a track button is the track reset, as before.

Scenes

A scene keeps the mutate of each track, so a scene plays the exact lists that you heard when you saved it.

On the Mixer pageResult
Shift + a scene padSaves the scene with each track's mutate.
A scene padPlays the scene with its saved lists. Stopped: at once. Playing: when the scene starts.
Duplicate + a scene pad, then anotherCopies the scene, its mutate too.
Clear + a scene padClears the scene, its mutate too.

A scene saved without a mutate plays its chains: a scene change ends the mutate of the tracks with no list in that scene.

Saving

When you save the project, it keeps the Mutate settings, each track's current mutate and the mutate of each scene. A project from an earlier firmware opens with the default settings.

Chapter 7

Track +: overview and layout

Track + is the track menu. It shows the eight knobs of the selected track on pages: the stock's own parameters and the extra parameters that Rhythm Plus adds, such as envelopes, LFOs, movement, glide and ring modulation.

  1. Select a track. Tap its track button again to open Track +.
  2. Tap a page pad (26-31) to choose a page. Each track keeps its own page.
  3. Turn a knob. The bar shows its value. Tap the track button again to close Track +.
PadsNameWhat they do
1-16StepsThe steps of the pattern, as on the track page. Hold a step to view or lock its values.
17-24BarThe value of the shown knob.
25Knob pickShows the page's next knob on the bar, without turning it.
26Parameter Editor (white)The stock's eight knobs of the track.
27-31Extra pages27 Filter (lime), 28 Mix (mint), 29 Pitch (sky blue), 30 none (dark), 31 FX (amber).
32AllWith Clear held: resets all pages (chapter 10).

Terms

TermMeaning
Shown knobThe last knob that you turned, or the knob that you picked with pad 25. Its light is in the track's colour.
EditTurn the knob to change the value for the whole track.
AutomateArm Record and turn the knob while the pattern plays. The movement is recorded into the steps.
LockHold a step, arm Record and turn the knob. The value goes into that step only.
ResetHold Clear and turn the knob right until it stops. The value goes back to its default; the knob light turns blue.
Delete automationHold Clear and turn the knob left until it stops. The knob's automation is deleted; the knob light turns red.

ToDo this

The extra pages (pads 27-31)

The extra parameters are kept for each track and saved with the project. You can view, edit and reset them; they have no automation and no step locks. Filter K7 and K8 are the stock's low-pass and resonance (white lights): you can automate and lock them, as on the Parameter Editor.

Shift on these pages: on a shape knob, or a rate knob in its synced half, Shift + turn right goes to the same pad on the second sweep, Shift + turn left goes back. On the other knobs, Shift + turn moves the value in fine steps.

Chapter 10

Resetting values and pages

One knob

Do thisResult
Clear + turn the knob right until it stopsThe value goes back to its default. The knob light turns blue.
Clear + turn the knob left until it stopsParameter Editor (and Filter K7 / K8): the knob's automation is deleted; the light turns red.

To reset the Pitch page's LFO fade-in (Shift + K7): hold Clear, then hold Shift, then turn K7 right. Press Clear first: Shift + Clear changes the metronome.

A whole page

Do thisResult
Clear + tap pad 26The eight stock parameters go back to their defaults, and their automation in the current pattern is deleted.
Clear + tap a page pad (27-31)All extra parameters of that page go back to their defaults.
Clear + tap pad 32All of the above: the stock parameters, their automation in the current pattern, and the extra parameters of all pages.

Page resets work on the selected track only. They do not change the sample, the mixer or the FX sends. The Filter page's K7 and K8 (the stock's low-pass and resonance) change only with pad 26 and pad 32, not with pad 27.
