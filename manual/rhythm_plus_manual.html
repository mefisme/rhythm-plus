Chapter 1

Pattern Mutate: overview and quick start

A mutate gives each track a new play list: a short run of that track's own patterns, in a random order. The track plays the list in a loop in place of its chain. Your patterns do not change. You can go back to the chains at any time.

Use it to find new variations of a groove: try a mutate, keep it if you like it, try another if you do not.

Quick start

  1. Press Patterns to show the Patterns page. Press Play.
  2. Hold Shift and tap Duplicate. Each track gets a new list. The lists start at each track's next pattern end.
  3. Tap again for another mutate. Each tap makes new lists.
  4. To go back to the chains: hold Clear and tap Duplicate.

The Duplicate button

On the Patterns page, Duplicate shows the state of the mutate:

DuplicateMeaning
Its normal lightNo button held: Duplicate copies patterns, as before.
Dim greenShift is held: a tap mutates.
Full greenShift + Duplicate is down.
Green, pulsingThe Mutate settings page is open.
RedClear is held and a track has a mutate: a tap ends every mutate.

The settings

Hold Shift and hold Duplicate for a moment to open the Mutate settings page. Tap Duplicate to close it. Shift + tap Duplicate on the page makes a mutate and keeps the page open. The settings are for all tracks and are saved with the project. Chapters 2 and 3 show each setting.

A track's mutate stays until you end it, pick a pattern for that track, or change the scene (see chapter 5).

Ending a mutate, scenes and saving

See which tracks are mutated

Hold Clear on the Patterns page. The buttons of the mutated tracks are green, the others dark. Duplicate is red when there is a mutate to end.

You doResult
Clear + a green track buttonThat track's mutate ends. Its button flashes red, then goes dark. The track goes back to its chain.
Clear + a dark track buttonNothing happens. The track is not selected.
Clear + DuplicateEvery mutate ends. The mutated tracks flash red. This also works on the Mixer, Grid FX, FX and Sidechain pages.
Clear held on the settings pageThe pads show the Patterns page, so you can see each mutate. Duplicate keeps pulsing green: you are still on the settings page. The pads do nothing. Down / Up show the other page of patterns. Clear + a track button ends that track's mutate.

On a track page, Clear + a track button wipes the track (chapter 12).

Scenes

A scene keeps the mutate of each track: its seed, the settings that made its list and its chain. So a scene plays the exact lists that you heard when you saved it, and Live goes on from them.

On the Mixer pageResult
Shift + a scene padSaves the scene with each track's mutate.
A scene padPlays the scene with its saved lists. Stopped: at once. Playing: at the end of track 1's pattern. The mutate plays until then.
Duplicate + a scene pad, then anotherCopies the scene, its mutate too.
Clear + a scene padClears the scene, its mutate too.

A scene saved without a mutate plays its chains: a scene change ends the mutate of the tracks with no list in that scene.

In a scene chain, the chain goes to the next scene when each mutated track has played the last slot of its list. Play starts on the chain's first scene, with its own lists.

With Pool With steps, a list uses only the patterns that have notes. If you add notes to an empty pattern, or clear a pattern, a saved list can change.

Saving

The project keeps the Mutate settings, each track's mutate and the mutate of each scene. A project from an earlier firmware opens with the default settings and no mutate.

Chapter 7

Step Mutate: overview and the settings page

Step Mutate changes the steps of the selected track's pattern: their notes, velocities, gates and more. It changes only the steps from the pattern's start step to its end step, so it works the same for 16 or 32 steps. Each track has its own settings.

Step Mutate changes the pattern itself. There is no undo. To keep the pattern as it is, duplicate it to an empty pattern on the Patterns page first, then mutate the copy.

Quick start

  1. Open a track's page.
  2. Hold Shift and tap Duplicate. The pattern's steps change places, as the stock Mutate does.
  3. Hold Shift and hold Duplicate for a moment. The settings page opens.
  4. Tap a target pad to turn it on or off. Hold it to see its settings on columns 6-8.
  5. Tap Duplicate to close the page. Hold Shift and tap Duplicate to mutate with the new settings.
PadsWhat they do
Pads 1-3, 9, 17, 18, 25-28The targets (chapter 8). Bright = on, dim = off. Tap to turn on or off. Hold to show the target's settings: they stay after you let go.
Columns 6-8The shown target's settings. A pad is one value. A meter fills from the bottom: more light, more change. A choice: the chosen value is full.
The other padsDark. They do nothing.

Pitch

Time stretch

Pads 9-16 change the length of the selected track's sample without a change to its pitch. The length follows the tempo. Pads 1 and 2 and the pitch knob change only the pitch.

PadWhat it does
9Stretch on or off. Green: on. Dim red: off. Off keeps the length (half bright) for the next on. With no length kept, on starts Follow.
10What fits the length. White: the whole sample. K2 and K3 then choose the part that plays, and it stays in time. Amber: the part from the start (K2) to the end (K3) fills the length.

Follow: no length is bright. The sample plays at its own speed at the tempo when Follow starts, and stretches with later tempo changes. The chosen length is bright and the other lengths are dim. A dim red pad is a length the track's sample cannot get to: it must play more than 4 times slower or faster. The pads show this as soon as you choose the sample. A flipped step's sample gets the same length. In Loop mode the stretched sample loops; in One Shot mode it plays one time; in Gated mode it stops at the end of the note. Each track keeps its own settings, and the project saves them.

You can turn stretch on and off while the sample plays. On: the sound goes on from where it is. Off: it goes on at the sample's own speed to the end of the note; the next note plays without stretch. Each change fades over about 5 ms, so it does not click.

On a Granular track, pads 11-16 set Scan, so the playhead moves through the part from K2 to K3 in that length, at the tempo. Tap the bright pad again: Scan goes back to the value it had before. If you turn Scan, the pad is no longer chosen. Pad 9 is dim red and pad 10 is dark. While Slice is on, pads 11-16 are dim red.

Chapter 13

Grid FX sends

Grid FX changes the whole mix. The sends let you keep some tracks out of it: a kick that stays clean while the rest of the mix stutters, for example. Each track has its own send.

The sends page

Do thisResult
Mixer or Grid FX: press ▼ to the pan knobs, then ▼ againThe sends page. The knob lights show each track's send.
Turn a knobThat track's send. Full: the track goes through Grid FX, as on the stock unit. Down to 0: Grid FX does not touch the track.
Clear + turn a knobOn the FX track: left to minimum, right to full, with the stock Clear turn threshold. Without an FX track: the send returns to full.
Press ▲Back to the pan knobs.
Grid FX, an effect held: press ▲ (lit in the effect's colour)That effect's settings. ▼ (lit amber) goes straight back to the sends.

The pads stay as they are: on the Mixer they mute tracks, on Grid FX they play the effects. So you can hold a Grid FX pad and turn the sends at the same time.

What a send changes

A send splits the track's sound: the part you send goes through Grid FX, the rest goes around it. The level, pan, mute, sidechain and master filter still work on all of the track. The shared delay and reverb returns go through Grid FX by default. The Shift controls (chapter 14) let you route either return around it.

While a beat repeat plays, a send that you turn up waits until the repeat ends: the track keeps playing live. The next beat repeat records it. A send that you turn down works at once.

The project saves the sends. A new project, or one from an earlier firmware, has every send at full.

One track can play Grid FX for you. Its steps turn the Grid FX pads on and off, so the effects play in time with the pattern, on the whole mix, as on the Grid FX page. The settings are the Grid FX page's, with the same values. Let go of all the pads and the knobs are the sends. The Track Parameter Editor does not open on the FX track.

Gates

A step's pads play for its gate. A later step does not cut them off: a long gate under the next steps plays together with them. A pad again on a step inside its gate (a step that its gate lights) plays on; on the first step after its gate, it starts again. One effect type has one engine, so for the same type the newer step takes over, with no restart (Beat Repeat goes from 1/16 to 1/4 and keeps playing). When its gate ends and the older gate still runs, the older pad comes back. If the older gate is over when the newer step starts, the effect starts again. A step has one pad of each type: a pad of a type that the step has goes in place of it, when you record or hold the step.

Saving

The steps' effects are saved with the project, apart from the track's own data, so the track's samples and flips do not change. A project holds 47 different sets of pads (two or more pads, or random or repeat); one pad on a step needs no set. When all 47 are in use, a step cannot get a new set and keeps what it had. A step deleted on any view loses its effects. Pattern copy and duplicate do not carry the separate FX assignments. Step Mutate moves assignments with their steps and automation, including multi-pad sets and random/repeat. Its Sample flip target changes Grid FX pads and respects Pink only / All steps.

The knobs are the sends

The FX track's K1-K8 are the sends of tracks 1-8, the same values as the sends page (chapter 13). Automate or lock them as on any track, and the sends follow the steps. A send turned on the sends page with Record on is recorded too, as the FX track's automation. In the Grid FX view, the pads that the FX track plays are bright.

The track has one set of knob automation: on the FX track it moves the sends, on a normal track the track's own K1-K8. When the track stops being the FX track, its knobs go back to the values they had before, and the sends page keeps the last sends. The project saves which track is the FX track.

Automation and saving

With an FX track enabled, K1-K3 record and play native automation on that track, from either view. The eight ordinary sends remain automatable too. Without an FX track, the three controls are saved global values. K4 is a manual setting saved with the project.

Silent editing

Audition off affects held pads on the sequencer track. It does not mute the sequenced effects, change their assignments, or prevent live performance from the Grid FX page. The global blend is independent: use it to fade the effects in or out while the music continues.

Clear and the lights

Hold Clear and turn left to minimum, or right to the default, using the stock 40-click threshold. Completing a turn clears that control's automation lane. K4's left/right choices are audition off/on. The knob colors stay visible while Clear is held; completion uses the native red/right-reset cues.

Both returns and the global blend default to full, retaining the previous sound. Existing project sends and FX assignments are preserved.

The playhead scrub

Hold Shift + Play to scrub with K1. K1 is green. K2 controls sound while stopped: green = on (the default), red = off. K3-K8 are dark and disabled while held.

The range and the display

Scrub passes across step pages, patterns and intermediate scenes. Only the beginning and end of the active range hold playback. A single 32-step pattern includes both pages. On a track's step view, the playhead pad is white and the displayed page follows it, including steps 17-32.

Sound and effects

Stopped audition uses the native step-preview path: normal sample playback, with no turntable effect or time stretching. With K2 on, each turn can audition the located step, including the first and last steps. Preview notes release when you let go or after about 120 ms without a turn, subject to the sample's play mode.

The FX track plays no sequenced effects while scrub is held, preventing irregular triggers during running scrubs. Shift's alternate pages do not open. Release restores normal knob controls. Ordinary Play starts from the song's start; ordinary playback still loops normally.
