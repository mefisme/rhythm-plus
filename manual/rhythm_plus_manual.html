Chapter 1

Pattern Mutate: overview and quick start

A mutate gives each track a new play list: a short run of that track's own patterns, in a random order. The track plays the list in a loop in place of its chain. Your patterns do not change. You can go back to the chains at any time.

Use it to find new variations of a groove: try a mutate, keep it if you like it, try another if you do not.

Quick start

  1. Press Patterns to show the Patterns page. Press Play.
  2. Hold Shift and tap Duplicate. Each track gets a new list. The lists start at each track's next pattern end.
  3. Tap again for another mutate. Each tap makes new lists.
  4. To go back to the chains: hold Clear and Shift, and tap Duplicate.

The Duplicate button

On the Patterns page, Duplicate shows the state of the mutate:

DuplicateMeaning
Its normal lightNo button held: Duplicate copies patterns, as before.
Dim greenShift is held: a tap mutates.
Full greenShift + Duplicate is down.
Green, pulsingThe Mutate settings page is open.
RedClear and Shift are held: a tap ends every mutate.

The settings

Hold Shift and hold Duplicate for a moment to open the Mutate settings page. Tap Duplicate to close it. The settings are for all tracks and are saved with the project. Chapters 2 and 3 show each setting.

A track's mutate stays until you end it, pick a pattern for that track, or change the scene (see chapter 5).

Chapter 3

The Mutate settings, page 2

Press Down on the settings page. Column 3 goes on with Length 5-8. Columns 4 to 6 are three more settings. The greener colours tell you that you are on page 2.

ColumnSettingPads, top to bottomWhat it does
3Length5, 6, 7, 8The Length bar goes on from page 1.
4AmountAll (default) · Most · Some · A littleHow much of the chain a mutate changes. The meter fills from the bottom up: more light, more change.
5RepeatsOn (default) · OffOff: a list never plays the same pattern twice in a row (also not from its last slot back to slot 1).
6Keep slot 1Off (default) · OnOn: each list starts on the chain's first pattern, a home beat. Only the slots after it change.

Amount, step by step

Each slot of a list either keeps the chain's pattern at that place, or gets a random one. For a chain of 4 patterns:

AmountSlots that changeExample: chain 1 · 2 · 3 · 4
All4 of 47 · 6 · 2 · 5
Most3 of 45 · 2 · 8 · 6
Some2 of 41 · 6 · 3 · 2
A little1 of 41 · 2 · 7 · 4

For other lengths the number is rounded, and at least one slot changes. Slots past the end of the chain are always random. With Keep slot 1 On, Amount counts only the slots after slot 1.

With Repeats Off, a track that has only one pattern with notes still gets a list. It plays that pattern.

Chapter 5

Ending a mutate, scenes and saving

See which tracks are mutated

Hold Clear on the Patterns page. The track buttons of the mutated tracks are green; the other track buttons are dark.

You doResult
Clear + a green track buttonThat track's mutate ends. Its button flashes red, then goes dark. The track goes back to its chain.
Clear + a dark track buttonNothing happens. The track is not selected.
Clear + Shift + DuplicateEvery mutate ends. Duplicate is red while Clear and Shift are held. The mutated tracks flash red.
Clear held on the settings pageThe pads show the Patterns page, so you can see each mutate. Duplicate keeps pulsing green, so you know that you are still on the settings page. The pads do nothing. Clear + a track button ends that track's mutate.

On a track page, Clear + a track button is the track reset, as before.

Scenes

A scene keeps the mutate of each track, so a scene plays the exact lists that you heard when you saved it.

On the Mixer pageResult
Shift + a scene padSaves the scene with each track's mutate.
A scene padPlays the scene with its saved lists. Stopped: at once. Playing: when the scene starts.
Duplicate + a scene pad, then anotherCopies the scene, its mutate too.
Clear + a scene padClears the scene, its mutate too.

A scene saved without a mutate plays its chains: a scene change ends the mutate of the tracks with no list in that scene.

Saving

When you save the project, it keeps the Mutate settings, each track's current mutate and the mutate of each scene. A project from an earlier firmware opens with the default settings.

Track +: overview and layout

Track + is the track menu. It shows the eight knobs of the selected track on pages: the stock's own parameters, the Pitch page, the Granular page and the FX page.

  1. Select a track. Tap its track button again to open Track +.
  2. Tap a page pad: 26, 29, 30 or 31. Each track keeps its own page.
  3. Turn a knob. The bar shows its value. Tap the track button again to close Track +.
PadsNameWhat they do

The Pitch page (pad 29)

The Pitch page moves the pitch of each note while it plays. It has four parts: an envelope that bends the pitch at the start of the note, a vibrato (the LFO), a random jump for each note, and a glide from the last note.

KnobParameterWhat it doesShift + knob
K1Envelope amountHow far the envelope bends the pitch. Right of the middle: the pitch starts high. Left: it starts low. Middle: off.Fine
K2Envelope shapeThe path of the bend: 16 shapes (chapter 10).2nd sweep
K3Envelope decayHow long the bend takes. Left: a fast click. Right: a slow sweep.Fine
K4Vibrato depthHow far the vibrato moves the pitch. Left: off.Fine
K5Vibrato shapeThe wave of the vibrato: 10 shapes (chapter 10).2nd sweep
K6Vibrato rateRight of the middle: locked to the tempo. Left: free speed.2nd sweep / fine
K7Random pitchEach note starts at a random pitch, up to 12 semitones up or down. Left: off.Vibrato fade-in
K8GlideThe pitch slides from the last note to the new one, up to about 1 second. Left: off.Fine

Shift on this page

On a shape knob (K2, K5), or on K6 in its synced half, Shift + turn right goes to the same pad on the second sweep, and Shift + turn left goes back. On the other knobs, Shift + turn moves the value in fine steps.

Shift + K7 is the vibrato fade-in: at each note the vibrato starts at 0 and grows to the K4 depth, up to 2 seconds. K7 lights tan while Shift is held.

The Pitch page's values are kept for each track and saved with the project. They have no automation and no step locks.

Chapter 12

Granular: what it does

The Granular page plays the track's sample as many very short pieces called grains. Each grain is a few milliseconds to half a second long, with a soft fade in and out. Many grains together make a new sound from the sample: a cloud, a drone, a stutter, or a chopped beat.

You choose where the grains come from (Position), how long they are (Size) and how many play (Density). Scan moves Position through the sample by itself. Scatter adds random changes to each grain. Slice, Lock and Timing turn the cloud into a rhythm.

Quick start

  1. Select a track that has a sample. Tap its track button again to open Track +.
  2. Tap pad 30. The Granular page opens and pad 30 lights lime.
  3. Hold pad 30. Pads 17-22 show the settings. Tap pad 17 until it is green: Granular is on.
  4. Still holding pad 30, tap pad 18 for amber: Texture plays all the time while the pattern plays. Let go of pad 30.
  5. Press Play. Turn K2 Size, K3 Density and K4 Scan, and listen.
After a reset, K4 Scan is a little right of the middle, so Position moves slowly forward. Turn K4 to the middle to stop it.

Granular: the Shift knobs

Hold Shift on the Granular page. K1-K6 show the second set of knobs in lime, and the bar shows the turned knob in lime. K7 and K8 are dark.

KnobParameterWhat it does
Shift + K1LevelThe volume of the grains, in fine steps. Default: the middle.
Shift + K2Size scatterEach grain is a random amount shorter. Right: up to its whole length.
Shift + K3TimingMiddle: off, Density spaces the grains. Left: the grains come at a fixed time apart, from 0.5 s near the middle to 6 ms at the left. Right: the grains come on the tempo (chapter 16).
Shift + K4JitterMoves each grain earlier or later, up to one gap between grains. It loosens a strict rhythm.
Shift + K5SliceLeft: off. Right: cuts the sample into 2 to 16 equal parts. Each grain starts at the start of a part (chapter 16).
Shift + K6LockLeft: each grain is new. Right: the same 16 grains repeat in a loop. Between: some grains change each time round (chapter 16).

Chapter 16

Granular: Timing, Slice and Lock

Timing (Shift + K3)

Right of the middle, each grain starts on a step of the tempo: from one grain each 1/2 note to one each 1/32 note. Density then sets the chance that a step plays: at the left only a few steps play, at the right every step plays. Low Density can leave a few steps in a row with no grain, so the sound stops for a moment. Turn Density up to fill the gaps.

Slice (Shift + K5)

Slice cuts the sample into equal parts and makes each grain start at the start of a part. On a drum loop cut to whole bars, the grains then start on the beat, not between the hits. Slice cuts the sample, not the grains: each grain keeps the length that K2 Size sets. To play one whole hit for each grain, set Size to about one part.

With Slice onResult
K1 PositionJumps from part to part. It does not slide.
K5 Position scatterEach grain starts on a random part.
K4 ScanSteps one part at a time, on the tempo. Middle: stop. Right: forward. Left: backward. Further from the middle: faster, from one part each 1/2 note to one each 1/32 note.

For a 2-bar loop cut into 16 parts, each part is an eighth note. Set Scan to step one part each 1/8 note, and the loop plays back in order, in time. Then add Position scatter to jump between the parts.

Lock (Shift + K6)

Lock repeats the random choices of the grains: position, pitch, length, timing and reverse. At the right, the same 16 grains play again and again. In the middle, some grains change each time round, so the pattern changes slowly. Lock needs something random to repeat: turn up K5, K6, Shift + K2 or Shift + K4.

With Timing on the tempo, 16 grains at 1/16 make one bar. Lock at the right then repeats a one-bar pattern. For the clearest loop, set K4 Scan to the middle.
